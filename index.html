    Positioning
    This is a content-based risk signal, designed to plug into an existing fraud stack — not replace it. It runs on the decoded QR payload alone (domain structure, typosquat similarity, entropy, accompanying text) with no live network calls required, so it can sit inline in a scan-to-pay or QR check-in flow with near-zero latency. A production integration would add live threat-intel feeds and transaction-context signals (new payee, unusual amount, first-time scan location) on top of this layer, in partnership with a bank or fintech's existing risk data.
    Prototype demo — content-only heuristics, not yet trained on confirmed fraud/legit labels from a live production dataset.
